Jack of all trades, master of one

High school made me fall for technology that heals things, and I have been chasing that ever since. With my interdisciplinary interests, I pursued a bachelor’s in systems and biomedical engineering. I wandered widely through clinical engineering, embedded systems, data science and software, then dug deep into AI for biomedical applications, especially neuroscience and neuroengineering. My aim is to build AI that helps scientific discovery in the medical and neuro fields, which in turn requires the models themselves to be robust, interpretable and trustworthy.
